# Delete your Koinz account or data

Koinz is developed by Kenny Lee. You can delete your Koinz account, or only some of your data, at any time.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open **Koinz** and sign in.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

## Delete only some data (keep your account)

- Delete a single entry from its menu in the **Ledger** tab.
- Delete a wallet and its transactions from the wallet's edit page.
- In the **Profile** tab, use the data deletion options to clear all bookkeeping data while keeping your sign-in.

## Without the app

Email [lee.mh.kenny@gmail.com](mailto:lee.mh.kenny@gmail.com) from the Google account you use with Koinz, with the subject "Delete my Koinz account" (or "Delete my Koinz data"). We act on the request within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in account (email address and user ID), when you delete the account
- Wallets, transactions, categories, split bills, projects, assets and notes you created
- Your household memberships and invites

## What may be kept

- Subscription purchase records kept by Google Play and RevenueCat, as required for billing and tax.
- Encrypted backups held by our database provider (Supabase), which expire within 30 days.

Shared household entries that other members created stay in their own ledgers.

## 刪除 Koinz 帳號或資料

- **刪除帳號：** 個人 → 刪除帳號 → 確認。
- **只刪除部分資料：** 在帳本刪除單筆記錄、在錢包編輯頁刪除錢包，或在「個人」清除所有記帳資料但保留帳號。
- **不用 App：** 用你登入 Koinz 的 Google 帳號寄信到 [lee.mh.kenny@gmail.com](mailto:lee.mh.kenny@gmail.com)，我們會在 30 天內處理並回信確認。
- **會刪除：** 登入帳號（刪除帳號時）、你建立的錢包、交易、分類、分帳、專案、資產與備註，以及家庭成員資格。
- **可能保留：** Google Play 與 RevenueCat 的訂閱購買記錄；Supabase 的加密備份會在 30 天內過期。
