# Delete your Koinz account

Koinz is developed by Kenny Lee. You can delete your Koinz account and its data at any time.

## In the app

1. Open **Koinz** and sign in.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

## Without the app

Email [lee.mh.kenny@gmail.com](mailto:lee.mh.kenny@gmail.com) from the Google account you use with Koinz, with the subject "Delete my Koinz account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted

- Your sign-in account (email address and user ID)
- Wallets, transactions, categories, split bills, projects, assets and notes you created
- Your household memberships and invites

## What may be kept

- Subscription purchase records kept by Google Play and RevenueCat, as required for billing and tax.
- Encrypted backups held by our database provider (Supabase), which expire within 30 days.

Shared household entries that other members created stay in their own ledgers.

## 刪除 Koinz 帳號

- **App 內：** 個人 → 刪除帳號 → 確認。
- **不用 App：** 用你登入 Koinz 的 Google 帳號寄信到 [lee.mh.kenny@gmail.com](mailto:lee.mh.kenny@gmail.com)，主旨「Delete my Koinz account」，我們會在 30 天內刪除並回信確認。
- **會刪除：** 登入帳號、你建立的錢包、交易、分類、分帳、專案、資產與備註，以及家庭成員資格。
- **可能保留：** Google Play 與 RevenueCat 的訂閱購買記錄（帳務與稅務需要）；Supabase 的加密備份會在 30 天內過期。
